Install Ledgerly on Mobile

  1. iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button in the browser bar, scroll down, and tap Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome / Edge): Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner and select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Test Offline Mode: Once opened from your Home Screen, switch on Airplane Mode — your ledger and budgets continue working locally.
